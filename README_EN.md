Arcanism is a magic Mod that blends technology and programming elements. At its core, it focuses on the production and consumption of Mana, building up a complete mana system.

> The mod is currently in early development. Some features may only exist in prototype form, and the features described below may only be partially implemented.

## Highlights

- "Factorio"-style multiblock machines and production lines;
- A four-element mana system where production requires balancing each element;
- Diverse physics/first-principles based ways to produce and consume mana;
- A "Noita"-style spell programming system;
- Modular wands, letting you customize your own casting style;
- Realistic production lines and numbers, without "balance" problems.

## The Mana System: How to Play?

The four types of mana are based on real-world physics, and each type can locally alter the corresponding physical quantity. Try gathering energy in out-of-the-box ways, then collect that mana with arcane materials! Thanks to this, each mana has many unique production methods, and you can even convert one mana into another (via physics, of course).

Each mana has its own uses, its own unique machines, and with the overclocking mechanic you can boost machine speed up to 5x!

## Spell Programming: How Does It Work?

Spell programming should not just be arranging spells by category — it should be a Turing-complete computing machine to truly achieve "a thousand players, a thousand spells." That's why we designed complete delay, computation, condition, and loop spells, letting you truly control every behavior of your spells.

Struggling with debugging? The wand has a built-in debugger showing the currently executing spell in real time, with auto breakpoints, single-step execution, tick-by-tick execution, continue/pause... everything you want! The wand also has a convenient edit/import/export system, delivering an IDE-level editing experience whether on the wand or in VSCode.

## Don't Want Tech/Programming?

The tech and spell programming parts are fully independent, and most materials have both exploration and crafting acquisition routes. No need to compromise — find your own arcane experience.

## Versions & Dependencies

Arcanism requires GeckoLib as a dependency and runs on version 26.1.2.
